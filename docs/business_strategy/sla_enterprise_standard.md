# Service Level Agreement (SLA): Enterprise Standard

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") sets forth the performance metrics and support commitments for clients subscribed to the **Enterprise Plan** of the **harikerja HRMS** platform.

---

## 1. Service Availability (Uptime)

harikerja HRMS guarantees a monthly uptime percentage of **99.9%** (Three Nines).

### 1.1 Calculation
Uptime is calculated as the total number of minutes in a calendar month, minus the number of minutes of **Downtime** experienced, divided by the total number of minutes in that month.

### 1.2 Exclusions
Downtime does not include:
*   Scheduled Maintenance (communicated at least 48 hours in advance).
*   Issues caused by the Client's hardware or internet connectivity.
*   Force Majeure events (natural disasters, nationwide ISP outages, etc.).

---

## 2. Priority Support & Response Times

Enterprise clients receive access to a dedicated support channel with guaranteed response times based on the **Severity Level** of the issue.

| Severity Level | Definition | Response Time (SLA) | Resolution Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S1 - Critical** | System Down: Core functions (Login, Attendance, Payroll) are unusable for all users. | **< 1 Hour** | < 12 Hours |
| **S2 - Urgent** | Major Malfunction: A critical feature is failed for a group of users, but workarounds exist. | **< 4 Hours** | < 24 Hours |
| **S3 - Normal** | Minor Bug: Cosmetic issues or individual user errors. | **< 12 Hours** | < 3 Business Days |

---

## 3. Dedicated Account Management

Subscribers to the Enterprise Plan will be assigned a **Dedicated Account Manager (DAM)**.
*   The DAM serves as the primary point of contact for strategic reviews, feature requests, and escalation of support tickets.
*   Quarterly business reviews to ensure the platform meets the client's evolving HR needs.

---

## 4. Service Credits

If harikerja fails to meet the Uptime Guarantee, the Client is entitled to **Service Credits** applied against their next monthly or annual invoice.

| Monthly Uptime | Service Credit Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **99.5% - 99.8%** | 10% of Monthly Fee |
| **99.0% - 99.4%** | 20% of Monthly Fee |
| **Below 99.0%** | 50% of Monthly Fee |

---

## 5. Maintenance Windows

*   **Weekly Maintenance**: Occurs every Sunday between 02:00 AM – 04:00 AM (WIB).
*   **Emergency Maintenance**: Reserved for critical security patches. harikerja will notify the DAM via WhatsApp/Email at least 15 minutes prior to the window.
