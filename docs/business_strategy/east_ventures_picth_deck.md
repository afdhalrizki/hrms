Below is the slide outline:

### 1. Slide 1: Cover & Vision
*   **Title:** HariKerja HRMS
*   **Subtitle:** Democratizing HR Digitization for UMK, UMM, and Enterprise Tiers with Flat-Tier Pricing.
*   **Key Message:** A modern, secure, and multi-tenant SaaS platform aiming to digitize 1.5 Million active users by keeping enterprise-grade features affordable.

### 2. Slide 2: The Problem
*   **The Scaling Penalty:** Current HRIS market leaders charge expensive per-employee fees. Costs grow linearly, penalizing business expansion.
*   **Spreadsheet Dependency:** Over 80% of businesses rely on manual spreadsheets to manage attendance and permissions.
*   **Compliance Risks:** Manual calculations for BPJS contributions and PPh 21 TER tax rules are highly prone to errors.

### 3. Slide 3: The Solution
*   **Disruptive Flat Pricing:** Plans starting at Rp 125,000/month. Businesses scale without scaling software costs.
*   **Zero-Config Compliance:** Automated calculations for BPJS and the latest 2024 PPh 21 TER tax regulations.
*   **ESS Mobile App:** Flutter-based mobile app featuring smart geofencing, facial recognition check-in, offline resilience, and secure digital payslips.

### 4. Slide 4: Market Segmentation & Quotas
*   **Subscription Plan Strategy**
    *   *UMK Segment (Essential)*: Flat Rp 125k/mo | Max 100 seats limit.
    *   *UMM Segment (Professional)*: Flat Rp 350k/mo | Max 1,000 seats limit.
    *   *Enterprise Segment (Premium)*: Flat Rp 1.5M/mo | Max 999,999 seats limit.
    *   *Large Enterprise (Dedicated)*: Custom from Rp 5M/mo | 2,000+ seats, dedicated cloud instance.

### 5. Slide 5: 1.5M Active Users Scaling Scenarios
*   **Three Projections to 1.5M Managed Users**:
    *   *Blueprint Distribution*: 20.25k tenants, Rp 4.83B MRR (Rp 57.9B ARR) at 70-80% gross margins.
    *   *UMK Dominant*: 44.04k tenants, Rp 7.65B MRR (Rp 91.8B ARR).
    *   *Enterprise Focus*: 12.84k tenants, Rp 4.95B MRR (Rp 59.4B ARR).

### 6. Slide 6: Go-to-Market Strategy
*   **High-Intent Search Ads:** Target search keywords like *"Aplikasi Payroll Murah"*, *"Kalkulator PPh 21 TER"*, and *"HRIS Murah"*.
*   **Product-Led Growth:** Self-service signup and automated backend provisioning provide instant trial access, lowering customer acquisition costs (CAC).
*   **High Retention:** HRIS is highly sticky; once payroll and attendance structures are set up, customer churn remains extremely low.

### 7. Slide 7: Technology & Security
*   **Database Schema Isolation:** Schema-per-tenant isolation in PostgreSQL ensures 100% data privacy and security.
*   **Private Network Gateway:** Django Admin console is whitelisted at the Nginx level, accessible only via Private VPN (OpenVPN/WireGuard).
*   **Account Authentication:** Strict 6-digit TOTP Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for administrative operations.

### 8. Slide 8: Team & Call to Action
*   **The Funding Ask:** Seeking **Rp 3,600,000,000** for **12.5% equity** (Post-money valuation: **Rp 28,800,000,000 / $1.8M USD**).
*   **Runway**: 24-month runway to build Scenario B (Growth Setup), procure core work tools (MacBook Pro/Air & testing devices), and reach cash-flow positive status.
*   **Contact:** info@harikerja.web.id | [https://harikerja.web.id](https://harikerja.web.id) (QA environment, production launch at HariKerja.com)
