## 5. Risk Mitigation

1.  **Low Churn Priority**: HRMS is a "sticky" product; once payroll is set up, companies rarely leave. Maintaining a healthy **LTV (Lifetime Value)** is critical.
2.  **Scalable Architecture**: Using *Multitenancy* ensures that the infrastructure cost only raises linearly while revenue grows.
3.  **Self-Service UX**: By making the app easy to use, we keep the support team lean, preserving the 70% profit margin.
